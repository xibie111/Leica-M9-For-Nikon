The Nikon coefficient inventory in nikon_matrices.json was extracted from
LibRaw 0.22.1, src/tables/colordata.cpp, with the finite model inventory and
aliases from src/tables/cameralist.cpp and src/metadata/normalize_model.cpp.
tools/update_nikon_matrices.py is the extraction and audit script.

Upstream source: https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw/tree/0.22.1
Each input's exact URL and SHA-256 are recorded in nikon_matrices.json.
The bytes used here were recovered from the vendored LibRaw source in the
locally cached official rawpy-0.27.1 source distribution; its LibRaw version
header identifies version 0.22.1. No later-version byte identity is claimed.

Changes: extraction into JSON, explicit expansion of shared prefixes only
for known upstream models, addition of finite retail/EXIF generation aliases,
and retention of black/maximum values for audit without applying them.
The raw integer coefficients and XYZ-to-camera direction are unchanged.
Four-channel entries remain in the inventory; this does not add native
decoding or processing support for four-channel or non-Bayer input files.
The separate Nikon E880/E900/E990 simple_coeff matrix is retained under
additional_upstream_matrices with its original C++ float literals, 3-by-4
camera-to-LibRaw-rgb_cam direction and scale 1. It is audit data only and
is never substituted for the XYZ-to-camera matrices in the 93 main entries.
